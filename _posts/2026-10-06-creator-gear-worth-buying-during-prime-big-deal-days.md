You have forty tabs open, a cart full of gear, and no idea which of it you actually need. Sound familiar?

Here's the hard truth: most creators don't have a gear problem. They have a plan problem. A sale is only a deal if the thing you buy solves a real bottleneck in how you make content. So before you click anything, ask yourself three questions. What is the weakest part of my videos right now? Is it picture, sound, or light? What would I actually use every week?

Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days is running now. Most of the gear below carries a Prime Big Deal badge. A few items, like the Sony ZV-1F, are simply marked down below list price right now. I sorted them by the problem they solve, so you can skip straight to yours.

*Prices were checked on October 6, 2026 and change often, so confirm the price on Amazon before you buy.*

## If you can only buy one thing

Overwhelmed? That is okay. Start small.

1. Fix sound first. The [DJI Mic Mini](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DDL8WGH5) works with the phone you already own.
2. Fix light second. One [key light](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07L755X9G) changes how every video looks.
3. Then buy the camera. When you are ready, the [Sony ZV-1F](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BHKKHNHM) is my pick for a first one.

Gear does not make you a creator. Showing up does. Buy the one thing that removes your biggest excuse, then go make something for the people you are trying to reach.
