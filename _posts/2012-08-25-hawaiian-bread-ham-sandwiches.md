These little sandwiches are amazing!  
I made them for my bible study the other night and they were a big hit!

What you'll need:

*An oven on 350º*

**Sandwiches**:  
2 packages of Hawaiian rolls  
1 lb Shaved ham from deli  
12 Slices of swiss or provolone

**Dressing**:  
1 stick of butter  
2 Tbsp Dijon Mustard  
2 Tbsp poppy seed  
2 Tbsp worchestershire sauce  
1/4 Cup brown sugar  
1 Tbsp of Onion Flakes

Melt the butter. Mix in the rest of the dressing ingredients. Sit to the side.

![Slap yo momma good – Hawaiian Bread Ham Sandwiches](/assets/uploads/2012/08/20120824-194243.jpg)

Build the sandwich. I actually had bought the Hawaiian bread mini sub rolls and cut them in half and then I didn't have to cut them down the middle since they were made to be sub rolls anyway. I took my ham and just laid it on liberally. Don't worry about stacking an individual sandwich - you can cut them all up again at the end. After I put about six slices of cheese to cover the complete loaf of bread so I used 12 slices in all covering my two packs of rolls.

Then I put the tops back and covered them in dressing.

![Slap yo momma good – Hawaiian Bread Ham Sandwiches](/assets/uploads/2012/08/20120826-080839.jpg)

Then I covered them in foil and put them in the fridge for like 10 minutes. I heard you should do it over night or not at all so whatever is best for you.

After that I put them in the oven, still covered, for 15 minutes and voila! Hawaiian bread ham sandwiches!
