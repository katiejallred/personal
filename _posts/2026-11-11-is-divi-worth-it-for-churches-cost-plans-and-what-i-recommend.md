## Do you need Divi Pro?

Probably not at first. Pro adds Divi AI, Divi Cloud (store your layouts and use them on any site), VIP support with faster response times, and team seats.

It's worth it if:

- You manage **several sites** (church, school, camp) and want to share layouts between them.
- You have **multiple staff** building pages and want team access.
- You want **AI help** drafting copy and images right in the builder.

If you're a team of one with one website, start with Divi and add Pro later if you need it.

## Divi vs. a church website platform

There are good church-specific website platforms out there. They host your site, handle updates, and often include church features like sermon media and giving integrations, for a monthly fee.

So how do you choose? Here's the contrast:

- **A church platform** is like renting a furnished apartment. Easy to move into, someone else fixes things, but you pay every month and you can only rearrange so much.
- **Divi on WordPress** is like owning a house. You pay once for the tools, you can build anything, and you can move hosts whenever you want, but someone has to mow the lawn: hosting, backups, and updates.

Neither is wrong. The right answer depends on your team.
