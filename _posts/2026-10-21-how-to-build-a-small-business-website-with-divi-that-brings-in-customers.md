## Build the pages that bring in calls

**Homepage.** One sentence that says what you do and where: "Family-owned HVAC repair serving Nashville and Williamson County." Then one button. Pick the action that makes you money, whether that's Call, Book, or Get a Quote, and make it the biggest thing on the page.

**Services page.** One section per service, in your customers' words, not industry jargon. Use Divi's blurb module with an icon, a short title, and two sentences each.

**About page.** People buy from people. Put your real face on this page. Tell them how long you've been doing this and why you care.

**Reviews.** Pull your best reviews into Divi's testimonial module, with names (with permission). Put a few on the homepage, not just a reviews page.

**Contact page.** Phone, email, hours, service area, a map, and a short form. On mobile, make the phone number a tap-to-call link.

## Get found locally

A beautiful website nobody finds doesn't help. A few basics go a long way:

1. **Match your details everywhere.** Your business name, address, and phone number should be identical on your website, Google Business Profile, and directories.
2. **Link your website from your Google Business Profile.** It's the first place local customers look.
3. **Name your towns.** Mention the cities and neighborhoods you serve on your homepage and services page, naturally.
4. **Ask for reviews.** Every happy customer, every time. Then feature them on your site.

## Start small

You don't need twenty pages. Build a homepage, a services page, and a contact page this week. Add the about page and reviews next week. A clear, simple site that's live beats a perfect one that's still "coming soon."
