## Start with your story, not your org chart

Here's the hard truth: nobody gives to an organization. People give to a story they want to be part of.

Your homepage should make the person you serve the hero, and invite the visitor to be the guide who helps. Before you change a single word in that layout pack, write three sentences:

1. **The problem.** Who is hurting, and how?
2. **The solution.** What you do about it, in plain words.
3. **The invitation.** What the visitor can do today.

Clear, not cute. "We provide weekend meals to kids in our county who would otherwise go hungry" beats "Nourishing futures, one bite at a time" every time. Use your real numbers, and only numbers you can back up.

## Build the five pages that do the work

**Homepage.** Put a real photo at the top (with permission from the people in it), your three sentences, and two buttons: "Give" and "Volunteer." Use Divi's number counter module to show impact you can verify, like meals served or families housed.

**Donate page.** This is the page that pays the bills, so remove every distraction. Embed the donation form from the giving platform you already use, or add a WordPress donation plugin, and let Divi style the page around it. Tell donors exactly what their gift does, but only if it's true for your program.

**Impact or stories page.** Build a Theme Builder template for stories once, then add a new one every month. A photo, a short story, a quote. Over a year, that page becomes your best fundraising tool.

**Volunteer page.** List your roles, the time commitment, and a simple sign-up form. Divi's built-in contact form works for simple sign-ups.

**About page.** Your team, your board, and your financial accountability. Donors want to know their money is stewarded well. Link to your annual report or your public financial filings.

Most first-time visitors won't give on their first visit. That's okay. Your goal is to keep the conversation going.

Every Elegant Themes membership includes **Bloom**, an email opt-in plugin, so you don't need to pay for a separate popup tool.

Add a simple "Get one story a month" signup to your stories page and footer, and connect it to your email platform. A monthly email with one story and one ask will outperform a website that just sits there waiting.

## Keep it fresh without burning out

A nonprofit website dies the day the one person who knew how to update it leaves. Don't let that happen to yours.

- **Set global styles once.** Define your colors, fonts, and buttons in Divi before you build, so every new page looks right automatically.
- **Save reusable sections.** Save your donation call-to-action section to the Divi Library and drop it at the bottom of every page.
- **Write it down.** Keep a one-page "how to update our website" doc with screenshots. Train two volunteers, not one.

## Start small
