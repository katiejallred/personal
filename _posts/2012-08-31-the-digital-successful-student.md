Here's a list of resources for tutoring and learning online for free. I've been using some of these since around 2004. Hope they help someone else!

### K12 and College

- [Alabama Tutoring](http://aplsnew-web.apls.state.al.us/aplsnew/patrons/homework/index.html "Alabama Tutoring") - Only Alabama Residents. Free Tutoring - Math - Science - Social Studies - English
  Grades K3-12, College Intro, and Adult Learner. *Pretty great site that has helped me through some difficult math problems and English papers!*
- [Yay Math!](http://www.yaymath.org/ "Yay Math!") - Algebra Math Help
- [Khan Academy](http://www.khanacademy.org/ "Khan Academy") - covers K-12 math, science topics such as biology, chemistry, and physics, and even reaches into the humanities with playlists on finance and history. They recently added Computer Science!
- [Patrick](http://www.youtube.com/user/patrickJMT "PatrickJMT") - Youtube guy who is great at explaining math!

### Computer Science

- [Code Academy](http://www.codecademy.com/#!/exercises/0 "Code Academy") - Learn Javascript, python, and JQuery! They're adding more courses soon
- [Khan Academy](http://www.khanacademy.org/ "Khan Academy") - Recently added computer science

I also need to stress the power of a good Youtube search because it's crucial to finding some great learning videos.
