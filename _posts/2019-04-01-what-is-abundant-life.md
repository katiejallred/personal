*Updated September 2026 with the context of John 10:10, Scriptures to study, reflection questions, and answers to common questions.*

Jesus said, if we follow him then we would have abundant life. What does that look like to you?

For me, it’s knowing my life serves a greater purpose—His purpose. I don’t have to worry about tomorrow because it’s gonna worry about itself.

He has my days planned for me. I can trust him. Rest in him. Believe that when he says he’s with me and that he’s for me, that he ain’t lying because he hasn’t failed me.

Jesus is perfect in all his ways, I just have to trust that his ways are better than my ways. I give up a little (my life) and I gain a lot (Jesus).

Having abundant life means I ain’t ashamed anymore. I don’t carry around these burdens or worries. Death has no grip on my life. God has my days numbered from the beginning and I just want to know that when my day is up that I was in God’s will and living his plan.

That’s abundant. It ain’t perfect. It isn’t stress-free. It’s not without suffering. But it isn’t without purpose and joy and grace and all those things I truly do not deserve.

Can I get a witness?

## Where Jesus talks about abundant life

The phrase comes from John 10:10. Jesus is teaching about Himself as the Good Shepherd. He says the thief comes only to steal, kill, and destroy, but He came so His sheep could have life, and have it in abundance. A few verses later He says the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep (John 10:11).

That context matters. Abundant life isn't something you earn by getting your act together. It's something the Shepherd gives, and He paid for it with His own life.

## Abundant life is not the easy life

It's tempting to hear "abundant" and think money, comfort, or a life where nothing goes wrong. That's not what Jesus promised. He told His disciples they would have trouble in this world (John 16:33). He said whoever wants to save their life will lose it, and whoever loses their life for His sake will find it (Matthew 16:25). That's the trade I was talking about: give up a little, gain a lot.

Abundant life is full, not easy. It's full of purpose, because you belong to Someone. It's full of grace, because you don't have to earn it.

## Scriptures to study

If you want to study this further, here are passages that line up with each part of this post:

- **Not worrying about tomorrow:** Matthew 6:34
- **Your days planned by God:** Psalm 139:16
- **God is for you:** Romans 8:31
- **He is with you:** Matthew 28:20
- **His ways are higher than yours:** Isaiah 55:8-9
- **No more shame:** Romans 8:1
- **Death has lost its sting:** 1 Corinthians 15:55-57
- **To live is Christ and to die is gain:** Philippians 1:21

Read one a day this week and ask what it tells you about the life Jesus offers.

## Questions to sit with

- What does abundant life look like to you right now?
- Where are you still carrying shame or worry that Jesus has already carried?
- What would it look like to trust that His ways are better than yours this week?
- Are you measuring your life by comfort, or by purpose?

## Live it today

You don't have to wait for a perfect season to have abundant life. It isn't stress-free, and it won't be without suffering. But it is full of Jesus, and that's enough. Follow the Shepherd today. Trust Him with tomorrow.
