Online communities are a great way to find like-minded people and share your passions. You can chat with friends, join groups, or start your group on any topic you love. Share photos and videos of what you're up to with the world. And if you need advice about anything at all, there's always someone available for support.

It is hard to believe that online communities are only a few decades old. Yet, these online spaces have grown and transformed into the powerful entities they are today, with billions of people across the world participating in them daily. This article will explore how online communities came to be and what makes them so unique.

## What is an online community, and how did it come to be

Online communities are online spaces that people from all over the world can collaborate and create content on. They are an online gathering place for people who share common interests to come together.

An online community is a space where the members can post, comment on, or like other users' posts; it's also possible to start your own discussion by posting a new topic in either individual discussions (in forums) or group discussions (in chat rooms). Some online communities are online forums for social activism or online support groups, while others provide a space to share thoughts and ideas on any topic.

Community online communities can be found in many forms of media content (e.g., blogs, online games) and other functionalities such as Facebook pages, YouTube channels, and even Reddit subreddits.

## The history of online communities

The first online community was Citizens' Band Radio, which came about in 1967. CB radio allowed users to communicate with each other via a set of different channels (or frequencies). These were open to anyone who wanted to join and talk at any time.

The next online community that came about in the 1980s, called Echo, was a worldwide online system that enabled people to type messages and exchange files without having any software installed on their computers. This first online communication network consisted of approximately forty users. The interface for this online space used what is now known as bulletin board systems (BBS).

This online community was followed by the video-oriented online social network MUDs, which came about in 1979 and were text-based. They used a virtual reality interface to allow users to create their own environment on-screen.

Then came online email communities, which were online spaces that enabled people to communicate through email rather than phone conversations. The first online community based on email came about in the late 1980s and was called CompuServe.

After email came internet forums or bulletin boards, these online spaces became popular in the 1990s. Users could post on a topic and have discussions with other people online, usually exchanging information or discussing topics of interest. Forums

The first online forum was called [The WELL](http://well.com), which came about in 1985. It offered users a place where they could have discussions on any topic that interested them, as well as meet like-minded people online. Forums still exist online today but are not as popular as they once were.

Then came online chat rooms in the 1990s that allowed people to talk to each other online through a text-only interface, like AOL Instant Messenger and ICQ before America Online Inc. Chat bought it is still very popular in online communities today apps providing online chat as a service.

In the 2000s, online communities continued to grow and evolve into systems that allowed people from all over the world, not just those who spoke English, to access them online through an interface in their own language. An example of this is Second Life—a virtual online environment with its own currency where users can create anything they want and interact online.

Blogs were created after forums. Users could leave comments on blogs that were interesting to them. Blogging online communities became popular because they allowed people to share their thoughts and ideas with the world.

Then finally, social media was created as the ultimate online community platform. The first social media website was Sixdegrees.com in 1997, but the more popular platform was Myspace.com in 2003. Myspace allowed users to create online profiles, upload photos, and videos, share music playlists and participate in online communities with other people.

Social media was the advent of the online community as we know it today. Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram are online spaces that allow users to connect through various social media posts. Facebook Groups are a great way to build an online community today.

These online communities have a wide range of features, including hashtags (#), private messages (DM), and live streaming video content. They are often accessed by using smartphones or tablets. Social media is the most popular online community online today.

## How have these communities changed over time?

The online community has evolved from a worldwide online system to online spaces that allow people in any language to access them. The first online communities were text-based and used bulletin boards, email, internet forums, chat rooms, blogs, and social media. As the years went on, these online communities began expanding with features like hashtags, private messages, and live streaming online video content. The online community has evolved into an online space that is accessed all over the world by many different people through their mobile devices.

## Why are people drawn to them in the first place?

People are drawn to online communities because they allow them to interact with others online. Online communities have become the ultimate online space for people from all over the world who want to share their thoughts and ideas or socialize in general. There is always something going on that makes it easy for people of any age to participate in these online spaces.
