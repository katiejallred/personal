## BONUS CONTENT:

**To find any Wordpress theme**, go to [Google.com](http://google.com) and type in:

"wp-content/themes/(theme-name)"

Make sure to use the quotations, because that's specific. Also the theme name will be the folder's theme name so the theme, "Twenty Fifteen" will be "twentyfifteen" instead.

Here's how to look for Divi. ---> "wp-content/themes/divi" or [here's a link to that search](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22wp-content/themes/divi%22&espv=2&biw=1100&bih=1278&ei=AYOIVc-KFImeNu2Pg7gB&start=0&sa=N).
