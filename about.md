When I was nine years old, I started a Harry Potter forum with some friends
from around the world. We had no idea what we were doing. We were just kids who
loved books and wanted to find other kids who did too. What we built, mostly by
accident, was a community: a place where people felt less alone.

I've spent my career since then trying to do on purpose what that forum did by
accident.

## What I do now

I help churches and nonprofits communicate clearly and use AI without losing
the human heart of their work. Most of the people I work with are a team of
one. They're writing the newsletter, running the social accounts, fixing the
website, and answering the email about the parking lot, usually all before
Tuesday. My job is to hand them a plan and give them back some time.

That work lives in two places:

- **Katie Allred Consulting** — strategy, workshops, and hands-on help for
  teams ready to stop winging it. The tagline says it best: *Automate the
  temporary. Invest in the eternal.*
- **[Allred Academy](https://academy.katieallred.com)** — AI education built
  for churches and nonprofits. Practical, plain-spoken, and usable the same day.

## Where I've been

- Former Meta and Adobe. My community work at Meta covered North America.
- Founded and sold ChurchCommunications.com, a community of 35,000+ church
  communicators.
- Author of *Church Communications* (B&H Academic) and *AI Prompts for Church
  Leaders*.

## Why it matters

Technology keeps changing. People don't. They still want to be seen, known, and
invited in. AI can take the repetitive work off your plate, like the tenth
version of the same announcement, so you can spend your hours on the things
only people can do. That's the idea behind everything I teach.
