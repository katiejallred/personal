## What I do now

I help churches and nonprofits communicate clearly and use AI without losing
the human heart of their work. Most of the people I work with are a team of
one. They're writing the newsletter, running the social accounts, fixing the
website, and answering the email about the parking lot, usually all before
Tuesday. My job is to hand them a plan and give them back some time.

That work lives in a few places:

- **Katie Allred Consulting** — strategy, trainings, websites, and community
  audits for teams ready to stop winging it. I've delivered more than 100
  trainings on communication, community, and AI. The tagline says it best:
  *Automate the temporary. Invest in the eternal.*
- **[Allred Academy](https://academy.katieallred.com)** — AI education built
  for churches and nonprofits. Practical, plain-spoken, and usable the same day.
- **Public Democracy** — as Director of Community, I build AI pipelines and
  lead community strategy for nonprofit partners.
- **Adobe** — I manage the Creative Cloud Discord community, more than 700,000
  members across Photoshop, Illustrator, Video, Firefly, and Express.

## Where I've been

- **ChurchCommunications.com** — I grew it from a single online group to a

## Teaching

I've taught at the University of Florida's College of Journalism and
Communications since 2016. I've also taught at Middle Tennessee State
University, Belmont University, and Ascent College, most recently on
generative AI in strategic communication.

## Why it matters

Technology keeps changing. People don't. They still want to be seen, known, and
invited in. AI can take the repetitive work off your plate, like the tenth
version of the same announcement, so you can spend your hours on the things
only people can do. That's the idea behind everything I teach.
