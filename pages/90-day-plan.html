What's inside

Four phases. Ninety days. One clear plan.

Every page is a worksheet you fill in, not a lecture. By day 90 you'll know what you have, what your people need, and what comes next.

  1. The channel audit

    List every channel your church uses, who has access, and what's working. Then Google your church the way a first-time guest would.

  2. Stakeholder interviews

    Who to talk to, the questions to ask, and the one question to ask your pastor so you know what a win looks like.

  3. Brand kit & logins

    Track down the logo files, colors, fonts, and every account login, and make sure no account depends on one person.

  4. Congregation survey

    Seven ready-to-use questions to learn how your people actually hear about what's happening, and what confuses them.

  5. Priorities with your pastor

    Turn your findings into recommendations, agree on priorities, and set up one simple way for ministries to send requests.

  6. The calendar & day-90 report

    Map the big moments of your year, plan the next thirty days, and close the loop with a one-page report for your pastor.

Is it a fit?

Built for the person who just got the keys.

This is for you if…

  • You just started as a church communications director or coordinator
  • You're a pastor, admin, or volunteer who inherited "communications"
  • Every ministry wants something promoted and you don't know where to start
  • You can't find the logo files, the logins, or the plan
  • You've been in the role a while and want a clean reset

Probably not for you if…

  • You want someone to redesign everything by next Sunday
  • You'd rather skip listening and go straight to changing things
  • Your church already has a documented strategy and calendar you're happy with
