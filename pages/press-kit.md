## OFFICIAL BIOS

## Use these bios as-is.

### Short Bio

Katie Allred is a community strategist, speaker, and author with 25+ years building online communities that have grown to hundreds of thousands of members.

She is the author of three books on church communications, AI, and social media, and has helped churches, nonprofits, and mission-driven organizations build communities people actually want to belong to.

### Long Bio

Katie Allred has spent more than two decades at the intersection of faith, technology, and community. She built her first online community in 1999 and has since grown audiences to hundreds of thousands across platforms, including leading community efforts at Adobe.

She is the co-author of *AI Prompts for Church Leaders* (2025), author of *Church Communications: Methods and Marketing* (B&H Academic, 2022), and co-author of *Instagram Posts That Worked*. Her work is used in universities and seminaries nationwide.

Katie holds an MA in Mass Communication from the University of Florida (4.0 GPA) and a BS in Computer Information Systems. She currently serves as Head of Community at Public Democracy and consults with organizations on communications strategy, AI adoption, and online community building.
