<p><strong>Information you provide directly:</strong></p>
<ul>

  <li><strong>Billing and mailing address</strong> — when you purchase a service or product</li>

  <li><strong>Messages and communications</strong> — anything you share through contact forms or scheduling tools</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Information collected automatically:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Usage data</strong> — pages visited, time on site, referring URL, clicks (via Google Analytics)</li>

  <li><strong>Cookies and tracking technologies</strong> — see Section 6 below</li>

  <li>To respond to your inquiries and support requests</li>
  <li>To process payments through Stripe, PayPal, Venmo, or TidyCal as applicable</li>
  <li>To analyze site traffic and improve content (via Google Analytics)</li>
  <li>To comply with legal obligations</li>
</ul>
<p>I do <strong>not</strong> sell, rent, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Occasionally I send sponsored emails on behalf of vetted partners — written and sent by me; your contact information is <strong>never</strong> shared with or transferred to the partner. You can unsubscribe at any time.</p>

</ul>
