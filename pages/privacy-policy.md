## Who I am

Katie Allred Consulting, LLC ("**I**," "**me**," or "**Katie Allred**") runs katieallred.com from Nolensville, Tennessee, USA. This policy explains what information I collect when you visit this site or buy from me, and what I do with it.

Send questions or requests through the [contact form]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}#message-title).

## What I collect

**Information you give me:**

- **Name and email address** when you join my email list, download a free resource, or send me a message
- **Anything you write in a form or booking question**, like details about your church, organization, or event
- **Billing details** when you buy a service or product. Card numbers are collected and processed by the payment company (Stripe, PayPal, Venmo, or TidyCal). I never see or store your full card number.

**Information collected automatically:**

- **Server logs.** The company that hosts this site (GitHub) records basic request data such as your IP address, browser, and the page you asked for, to keep the site running and secure.
- **Embedded tools.** Forms, the booking calendar, the email sign-up form, and videos load from the services listed under [Services I use](#services-i-use). When they load, those services receive your IP address and browser details and may set their own cookies.

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies of its own.

## How I use it

- To deliver what you bought, and to talk with you about it
- To answer your messages and schedule calls
- To send my email newsletter, updates, and the occasional sponsored email from a partner I've vetted. I write and send those emails myself; the sponsor never gets your information.
- To keep records I'm required to keep, like tax records

I do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and I don't share it with sponsors or advertisers.

## Legal basis (EU and UK visitors)

If you're in the European Union or United Kingdom, I rely on:

- **Contract**: to deliver services you've bought
- **Consent**: for my email list (withdraw it anytime by unsubscribing)
- **Legitimate interests**: to reply to messages you send and keep the site secure
- **Legal obligation**: to keep records the law requires

## Services I use

These companies handle information on my behalf. Each has its own privacy policy.

- **GitHub Pages** (site hosting): [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- **SendFox** (my email list): stores your name and email so I can send newsletters. The sign-up form uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam. [SendFox privacy policy](https://sendfox.com/privacy)
- **Tally** (contact and download forms): [Tally privacy policy](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy)
- **TidyCal** (booking calls, including [meetwithkatie.com](https://meetwithkatie.com)): collects your name, email, and booking answers, and payment if a session is paid. [TidyCal privacy policy](https://tidycal.com/privacy-policy)
- **YouTube** (embedded videos, in privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube doesn't set cookies until you press play): [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Stripe, PayPal, and Venmo** (payments): [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy), [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/privacy-full), and [Venmo](https://venmo.com/legal/us-privacy-policy/) privacy policies

## Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies itself. Cookies may come from:

- **Embedded tools** listed above, when they load or when you use them
- **Affiliate programs**, set by the retailer's site after you click an affiliate link, so the retailer knows I referred you (see my [affiliate disclosure]({{ '/affiliate-disclosure/' | relative_url }}))

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. Some embedded forms or the booking calendar may not work without them.

## How long I keep it

- **Email list**: until you unsubscribe
- **Purchase records**: 7 years, as US tax law requires
- **Messages and form submissions**: up to 2 years
- **Booking details**: up to 2 years after your call

## Your rights

**Everyone** can ask me to:

- Show you the personal information I have about you
- Correct anything that's wrong
- Delete your information (unless I'm legally required to keep it)
- Stop emailing you. The fastest way is the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.

**California residents (CCPA/CPRA)** also have the right to know what I collect and how I use it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (*I don't sell or share it*), and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

**EU and UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR)** also have the right to data portability, to restrict processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, send a message through the [contact form]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}#message-title). I'll respond within 30 days.

## Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you're 13 to 17, you're welcome to browse, but you'll need a parent or guardian to make a purchase. If you think a child has sent me personal information, let me know and I'll delete it.

## International visitors

This site is run from the United States. If you visit from somewhere else, your information will be processed in the US, where privacy laws may differ from yours.

## Changes to this policy

When I update this policy, I'll change the "last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, I'll let email subscribers know.
