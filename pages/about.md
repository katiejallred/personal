## My Story

## You have something worth sharing. Let's make sure the right people hear it.

---

I built my first online community in 1999. Back when the internet was still dial-up and nobody called it "community strategy." I did not have a framework or a playbook. I just knew that people needed connection, and I was stubborn enough to figure out how to create it.

Twenty-five years later, I have helped everyone from solo creators and thought leaders to Fortune 500 brands do the same thing. I co-founded ChurchCommunications.com, which grew to 35,000+ leaders before being acquired by Missional Marketing. I have managed communities for Adobe Creative Cloud on Discord (500K+ members), led partner development at Meta, and taught social media and AI strategy at five universities.

Here is what I have learned: most people are not struggling because their message is bad. They are struggling because they have not built the infrastructure for it to spread. The right community, built with intention, changes everything.

I wrote the book on it. Literally. *Church Communications: Methods and Marketing* (B&H Academic, 2022) is used in universities across the country. The principles don't stop at the church door. They work for any creator, leader, or organization that wants to build something people actually show up for. And I am still learning, still teaching, still building.
