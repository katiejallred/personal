Frequently asked questions

Do you work with small churches or only large ones?

All sizes. Whether you have one person doing everything or a full communications team, every engagement is scoped to fit your context.

We're on a tight budget. Is any of this affordable?

Yes. The Digital Secret Shopper is $399 and AI Basics is $500. For budget-conscious churches, those are often the best places to start: high value, low cost, and clear next steps. Church websites start at $7,000.

What does a "secret shopper" actually look at?

Digital: your website, social media, email, and online first impression. On-site: the full guest experience from the parking lot to after the service, seen through a first-timer's eyes. Either way, you get a written report with specific, actionable feedback.

How long do engagements take?

Digital Secret Shopper: 1–2 weeks. Communication Audit: 2–3 weeks. AI Policy Writing: about a week. Training workshops are usually scheduled within 4–6 weeks, and preaching or teaching depends on availability.

Can you work with churches outside Nashville?

Absolutely. Digital services are fully remote. On-site secret shopper visits and preaching or teaching require travel, which is included in or added to the quote depending on location.
