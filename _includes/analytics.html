{%- comment -%} Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js) with Google Consent Mode. The ID is `google_analytics` in _config.yml. Only production builds (JEKYLL_ENV=production, as in the Pages workflow) load it, so local previews don't count as visits. In the regions listed in `analytics_consent_regions` (_config.yml: the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland) analytics cookies stay off until the visitor accepts them in the cookie banner (_includes/cookie-banner.html); Google works out the visitor's region and sends only cookieless pings until then. Elsewhere they're on unless the visitor declines under "Cookie settings". A choice is stored in localStorage as `ka-consent` ("granted" or "denied") and wins everywhere. Ad signals are always denied: the site runs no ads. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if site.google_analytics and jekyll.environment == 'production' %} {%- endif %}
